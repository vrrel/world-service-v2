In the world of *Crystal Realms*, many players dream of becoming a sultan in a short time. Yet, only a handful can truly make it happen. One of them is **Lacielico**. He began his journey from absolute zero in early February 2026. Now, just three months later, his total assets calculated at their lowest possible prices have breached the staggering figure of **882,000 Realm Tokens**.

How did he do it? Did he find a glitch? Did he get lucky with an ultra rare item drop? Or is he a market wizard?

The answer might surprise you. There is no magic here. This is a story about a business blueprint, phase discipline, and a mentality forged in the fires of adversity. Here is our exclusive interview, dissecting his entire journey.

### The Early Phase: Observing, Choosing a Path, and Making Peace with "The Grind"

Like any other newcomer, Lacielico didn't jump straight into investing. He spent his initial time on a crucial step that many players skip: **in depth research and observation**.

"Yeah, in the very beginning, you definitely look around first to see what's in the game, its mechanics, and then the sources of income," he explained. "After that, you figure out where you fit in and what you want to focus on. Just focus and run it consistently."

His initial goal was also quite unique. He wasn't solely chasing virtual wealth. "My goal in playing this game was to fix a broken mindset. I apply it in the game first as a training ground, so after it's fixed, I can bring it back to real life," he admitted.

So, what concrete steps did he take when his pockets were completely empty? The answer is very grounded: **mining and collecting**.

"Since I had no capital at the start, I focused on mining, selling iron and coal, just gathering capital," he said. What's interesting is his work principle: "I don't set a time limit, because the principle is, don't limit your work hours if you're working for yourself."

It was at this point that the seeds of his sultanate began to sprout. He actively asked other players, including me (MyJob), about profitable opportunities. That's how he learned about the **EIJ (Eye In Jar)** , an item that became his main stepping stone. The reason was simple yet genius: "Because it can become a pocket shard, so if it doesn't sell well, you can use it yourself." An investment principle with a built in safety net.

### The 3 Phase Blueprint: Foundation, Expansion, and Realization

The most fascinating part of Lacielico's strategy is his neatly structured three phase system. He doesn't move randomly. This is the blueprint that brought him to 882K RT:

**Phase 1: The Base Asset Phase (Building the Foundation)**
This is the most critical phase. Instead of chasing immediate cash profit, he built permanent production tools. "Phase 1 is the base asset phase. Find base assets that you'll use throughout the game; this is the initial pillar of the foundation," he stressed.
What did he build? *Farms, drills, cauldrons*, and even a custom designed *world* with its own music. These assets are "money machines" that work for him 24/7. As he puts it, "A *drill* is a permanent asset, so it's *nothing to lose*. Just don't be lazy with your capital."

**Phase 2: The Expansion Phase (Mass Production)**
Once the foundation was solid, he entered the mass production phase. The focus was no longer on building tools, but on producing semi finished and finished goods. "Phase 2 is the expansion phase, where all base assets are complete, so you focus on collecting goods," he stated.
This is where he became a production engine. He processed his own raw materials: *Glowing Vines, Morels, Spruce, Sand* all turned into EIJ. He also made minor diversifications, like selling *Spruce Logs* or turning *Dungeon* results into *Goblin Armor*.

**Phase 3: The Market Timing Phase (The Pump & Realization)**
This is the stage of an executor. After stockpiling goods, he plays the market timing. "Phase 3 is the market timing phase, where your goods have piled up, you pump them, and realize the profits to rotate into the next assets. Just repeat," he explained matter of factly.
From this, we learn that he is not just a master producer but also understands precisely when to sell high and rotate his profits into more lucrative assets.

### Edge in the Market: Not Instinct, But Real World Science

How can he read the market so expertly? Lacielico reveals his competitive advantage: 5 years of experience in the real world money market.

"That comes from market experience. I've been in the real money market for 5 years, so when I got here, the market concept was super easy. It's just that many people don't get it. So, my 'school fees' were paid in real life," he explained.

Yet, he flatly rejects the idea that he relies on gut feeling. "Entering an investment using feeling is fatal. Being impatient is fatal," he stressed. For him, analysis is key. He shared his three pillars of analysis for free: **"Try to research fundamentals, season, and timing."**

---

### Forged in Fire: Bullied, Left Behind, and Rising Alone

His success today did not come smoothly. A bitter story became his fuel. When his gaming partner (me) left him for two months, he faced bullying from another player.

"I was going solo when you left me for 2 months. I got bullied by IGRIS," he recalled. However, he didn't hold a grudge. "I never hold grudges, I just remember it. The goal was set from the very start."

A real world setback was also a powerful driver. "My goal in playing this game was because I was almost wrecked, nearly at zero. So, I'm fixing my mindset, practicing it, and later, when I'm done, I'll return to real life to fight again." This game is his mental training camp.

### A Sultan's Message: This is Still "The Grind"

At the end of the interview, he humbly refused to be called successful. When asked about his current activities, his answer might slap those seeking an instant path:

"Until now, I'm still grinding. I call it work. It's just like that, a company boss still works, right? Even if you're a director, you don't just laze around at home every day."
His activities remain the same: producing *Glowing Vines, Morels, Spruce, Sand*, and processing them into EIJ.

His ultimate tip for all players is a refreshing, blunt reality: **"Every business is profitable as long as you are consistent every day. It's not magic."**

And finally, a profound reflection for all of us: "It depends on the player, whether they intend to get rich or play for fun. Not everyone who plays has to be rich."

---

Lacielico's story proves that in *Crystal Realms*, and perhaps in real life, there is no such thing as instant wealth. What exists are **a solid foundation, consistent work without time limits, and the ability to continuously learn from the market**.
