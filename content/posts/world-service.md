## 🔹 For Those Who Need Workers (Customers)

Have a project or task that needs to be done but don’t have the time or energy?  

At World Service, you can find ready-to-work workers with a simple and secure process.

### Advantages for Customers:

- ✅ **Just contact an admin** – Simply tell the available admin what you need.
- ✅ **Transparent pricing** – Admin only deducts a **5% tax** from your total payment. No more, no less.
- ✅ **Work guarantee** – The worker will complete the task first, then payment is forwarded.
- ✅ **Equipment lending available** – New players without tools? You can borrow equipment from the admin (if the admin is willing).
- ✅ **Feedback-friendly** – Have criticism, suggestions, complaints, or want to negotiate prices? Submit them through the **provided board** openly.

### Simple Ordering Process:

1. **Customer pays the admin** according to the agreement.
2. **Admin instructs the worker** to do the job.
3. After the job is done, **admin pays the worker** after deducting the 5% tax.

> 💡 A 5% tax is small, but it means a lot in keeping the system professional and admins motivated to serve you!

## 🔹 For Those Who Want to Become an Admin – A Hassle-Free Business Opportunity!

World Service also opens opportunities for you who want to **join the system as an admin**.  

This is a perfect opportunity for those who enjoy networking, managing people, and earning income from intermediary services.

### Why Become an Admin at World Service?

- 💰 **Income from 5% tax** – Every transaction you manage gives you a fixed commission. The more transactions, the bigger your accumulated earnings.
- 🤝 **Build your reputation** – The better your service, the more customers and workers will trust you.
- 🧰 **Help new players** – You can lend equipment to new players in need, making you a highly sought-after admin.
- 🗣️ **Receive direct feedback** – Customers can give criticism and suggestions through the board, so you know what to improve or negotiate.
- 🔄 **Full control over the intermediary system** – You become the trusted link between customers and workers.

Being an admin here means you are **building your own business** with the support of a clear and fair World Service system.
